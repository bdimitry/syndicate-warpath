# SyndiCate guide rules

## Visual assets

- Never create, embed, or use SVG, including inline SVG, SVG icons, sprites, data URIs or libraries that output SVG.
- Use raster images or HTML/CSS. Preserve agreed visual assets rather than replacing them with simplified programmatic icons.
- Use genuine game screenshots for interface instructions. Keep annotations separate from the original pixels and translate their explanations into all supported languages.

## Explain every recommendation

- Every page must explain why its recommendations help. Place the explanation beside the relevant action or stage, not only in a general introduction.
- Explain the practical benefit, the tradeoff or consequence of doing otherwise, and relevant exceptions. Do not repeat the instruction as its own justification.
- Cover individual research stages and parallel tracks, unit assembly and coupon rules, calendar days and Jurisdiction steps, and parts decisions. Preserve this coverage when adding or editing content.
- Apply the same standard to Russian, English, Vietnamese and Arabic. Keep Arabic RTL and screenshot coordinates unchanged.
- Treat the supplied progression order as SyndiCate's strategy, not a proven universal optimum. Do not invent game effects, probabilities, savings, costs, node names or guaranteed returns to justify it.
- Keep explanations understandable to players. Verify new game-mechanic claims with reliable evidence and retain source links where useful.

## Verification

- Build after changes and verify affected pages in all four languages at mobile and desktop widths.
- Check explanation coverage, navigation, language persistence, RTL, and existing screenshots when affected. Keep checks proportional to the change.
